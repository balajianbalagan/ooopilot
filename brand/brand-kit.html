01 — The mark

Three rings, one of them broken

OOO is an absence, so the mark is built around a gap. Three concentric forms read as the three O s of OOO and as an outgoing call signal at the same time. The break in the outer ring is the load-bearing detail — close it and the mark stops meaning anything.

03 — Construction

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the radius of the inner ring — one quarter of the mark’s width — clear on all four sides. Nothing crosses the dashed line: no type, no rule, no avatar stack.

Minimum size

At 16 px the outer ring drops away and the favicon carries the dot and one open ring. Below 16 px, use the core dot alone.

04 — Misuse

Four ways to break it

Don’t

Stretch or squash. Scale both axes together.

Don’t

Rotate. The gap sits at 3 o’clock, always.

Don’t

Draw it in a state colour. Green, amber and red mean something specific.

Don’t

Close the outer ring. Without the gap it’s a target, not an absence.

05 — Colour

One accent, six neutrals

The palette is dark-first because the product lives in Slack and a terminal. Signal Blue is the only brand colour and it is spent on actions — dialling, calling, confirming. The neutrals are slate biased toward the same hue, so nothing reads as unconsidered grey. Click a swatch to copy the hex.

06 — State colour

Three colours that are not brand colours

These carry the one distinction the whole product rests on: knowledge_items.verified. They are never used decoratively, never used on the logo, and never swapped for the accent.

Verified Jira-derived fact. source_type = JIRA, verified = 1. The agent may state it as fact on the call.
Claim Something a coworker said on the phone. source_type = CONVERSATION, verified = 0. Stays unverified until a human confirms it.
Discrepancy Jira and the call disagree. The only state that interrupts someone: PHX-121 reads In Progress, but Karthik said testing is complete.
07 — Typography

Archivo, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono

Archivo is the voice: slightly squared, engineered, confident at display sizes without being loud. Plex Sans handles everything that has to be read carefully. Plex Mono is reserved for things the machine produced — issue keys, JQL, timestamps, transcript turns — so monospace itself signals provenance.

08 — Voice

An assistant, never the employee

The product speaks in the first person as a proxy and says so within the first sentence of every call. It never impersonates the person who is away, never guesses beyond its context, and never presents a claim as a fact.

Call opening — spoken “Hi Raj, this is an AI assistant calling on behalf of Arun, who’s out of office this week. I have his Phoenix launch context — can I walk you through where it stands?”
Outside context — spoken “I don’t have that in Arun’s notes, so I won’t guess. I’ll flag it for him to answer when he’s back Monday — anything I should add to that?”
